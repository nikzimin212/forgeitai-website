What does the free IT audit include?

I review your current infrastructure — network, endpoints, Microsoft 365, backups, and security posture — then deliver a plain-English report of what's solid, what's at risk, and what it would cost to fix. No sales pitch, no invoice, no obligation.

How fast do you respond to issues?

Under 2 hours during business hours (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–6 PM PT). For urgent after-hours emergencies, text (503) 609-0554 with “URGENT” and I'll respond as soon as possible. Managed clients on 24/7 plans get continuous monitoring.

Can you help with CMMC / NIST 800-171 compliance?

Yes — that's a specialty. I've prepared live defense-sector environments for CMMC, not just studied the framework. Engagements start with a gap analysis, then documentation, policy creation, and a remediation roadmap.

Do you only serve Vancouver, WA and Portland?

Onsite work covers Vancouver, WA and the Greater Portland area. Everything else — monitoring, helpdesk, M365, security, AI automation — I deliver remotely for clients anywhere in the US.

Is my business data safe with AI tools?

Yes. I build on enterprise-grade AI platforms (such as Anthropic Claude) with commercial data protections, and I design every automation so your data stays in systems you control. Nothing gets used to train public models.
